AISSMS Campus Canteen – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

AISSMS Campus Canteen lets students order from campus canteens (COE and IOIT), pay online and collect their food with a pickup code. The service is operated by [operator name and address]. Contact for privacy questions: [privacy contact email].

What we collect and why

DataWhy
Name, email address and profile photo from your Google accountTo sign you in and show your account. Any Google account can be used.
Your chosen instituteTo show the right canteens.
Orders: items, prices, times, pickup and collection statusTo prepare and hand over your food, show your order history and receipts.
Payment references from Razorpay (order and payment IDs, amount, status)To confirm payments and process refunds. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details; Razorpay handles them.
Reviews, ratings and review photos you choose to postTo show other students what items are like. Photos are re-processed and their location/camera data is removed.
Reports you file about reviewsTo moderate abusive content.
Device name and sign-in session recordsTo keep you signed in securely and let you sign out other devices.
Technical logs (time, request type, status; no message contents or payment details)To keep the service running and investigate problems.

Who can see it

  • Staff of the canteen you order from see your order items and status. Counter staff only see the items they prepare.
  • Your reviews are public in the app with your first name only.
  • Razorpay processes payments and refunds under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting providers store data on our behalf. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

[To be decided by the operator, for example: orders and payment records for the period required by tax law; review photos until you delete the review or the canteen removes it; sign-in sessions until they expire (at most 90 days) or you sign out.]

Your choices

  • Edit your reviews or delete their photos in the app.
  • Sign out of any device from the app.
  • Ask for a copy or deletion of your data by writing to [privacy contact email]. Records we must keep by law (for example payment records) are kept for that period only.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Sign-in tokens on your phone are encrypted with a key held in the phone's secure hardware. Staff passwords are stored as one-way hashes.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and show the date above.
